The tiny server agent.

vio runs, debugs and secures your Linux servers over SSH. One small binary, any model, and nothing changes without your approval.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ThembinkosiThemba/vio/master/site/install.sh | sh
> irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ThembinkosiThemba/vio/master/site/install.ps1 | iex

Then run vio. One binary, nothing installed on your servers.
